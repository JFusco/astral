# Crypto execution, discovery, and deterministic eligibility

## Which crypto markets can ASTRAL research and execute for a Georgia account?

### Takeaway
Alpaca can support a considerably broader paper research universe than BTC, ETH and SOL, including meme coins. Discover assets dynamically and maintain a separate execution eligibility record: appearing in a catalogue or research shortlist is not permission to trade.

### Cited Findings
- Alpaca's March 2026 supported-pairs page includes USD pairs for BONK, DOGE, PEPE, SHIB, TRUMP and WIF. It also lists ADA, ARB, AVAX, DOT, FIL, HYPE, LDO, ONDO, POL, RENDER, SKY and XRP, among others. This is a documentation catalogue, not an account-specific live asset query. — [Supported cryptocurrencies](https://alpaca.markets/support/what-cryptocurrencies-does-alpaca-currently-support)
- The Trading API discovers crypto through `GET /v2/assets?asset_class=crypto`; fields include asset UUID, pair symbol, active status, tradability and crypto quantity/price increments. Crypto is non-marginable, cannot be sold short, and has a documented $200,000 per-order notional maximum. Crypto is offered throughout the day, seven days a week. — [Crypto spot trading](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/crypto-trading)
- Georgia is explicitly included in Alpaca's supported US jurisdictions. Account approval and applicable agreements remain account-specific. Do not infer wallet transfer eligibility from trading eligibility. — [Alpaca cryptocurrency support](https://alpaca.markets/support/alpaca-cryptocurrency); [Wallet eligibility](https://alpaca.markets/support/what-enables-a-user-to-be-qualified-to-use-crypto-wallets)
- Current crypto order documentation supports market, limit and stop-limit, with GTC and IOC; a July 2025 support page also lists FOK. There is a real documentation inconsistency. Use the intersection GTC/IOC until capability is verified, and do not assume crypto bracket, OCO, stop-market, trailing-stop or post-only support. — [Crypto orders](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/crypto-orders); [Support order types](https://alpaca.markets/support/what-type-of-orders-are-supported-for-cryptocurrency)
- The older fixed minimum table dates to November 2022. The coin-pair FAQ instead describes a dynamic minimum tied to a USD equivalent and says maximum accepted precision is nine decimals. Runtime metadata should outrank copied tables. — [Old minimum table](https://alpaca.markets/support/what-are-the-specific-order-limitations-for-a-given-crypto-pair); [Coin-pair FAQ](https://alpaca.markets/support/alpaca-crypto-coin-pair-faq)
- Kraken's public AssetPairs endpoint exposes minimum quantity, minimum cost, tick size, status and country-code filtering; it has optional canonical display names, avoiding legacy X/Z symbol aliases. Its current US geographic exclusions list Maine and New York, not Georgia, but individual asset/account restrictions still apply. — [Kraken AssetPairs](https://docs.kraken.com/api-reference/market-data/get-tradable-asset-pairs); [Kraken eligibility](https://support.kraken.com/articles/where-is-kraken-licensed-or-regulated)
- Coinbase Advanced product metadata includes increments, minimums and `cancel_only`, `limit_only`, `post_only`, `trading_disabled` and `auction_mode`. The Advanced sandbox returns static mocked responses, not live matching simulation. Actual current Advanced fees must be obtained for the account/product; older Coinbase Pro tables should not be substituted. — [Product metadata](https://docs.cdp.coinbase.com/api-reference/advanced-trade-api/rest-api/products/get-product); [Sandbox](https://docs.cdp.coinbase.com/coinbase-app/advanced-trade-apis/sandbox); [Advanced fees](https://help.coinbase.com/en/coinbase/trading-and-funding/advanced-trade/advanced-trade-fees)

### Inferences
- Start with Alpaca USD spot pairs to avoid collateral conversion, shorting and cross-currency bookkeeping complexity. Keep alternate-venue adapters disabled until independently tested. A second exchange may improve symbol coverage and venue liquidity, but adds balances, fee schedules, permissions, order state and reconciliation requirements; it does not automatically improve returns.
- Proposed discovery record, versioned and immutable per decision:
  `venue`, `account_region`, `execution_location`, `asset_uuid`, `canonical_pair`, `base_id`, `quote_id`, `asset_name`, `asset_class`, `status`, `tradable`, `metadata_received_at`, `metadata_hash`, `min_qty`, `qty_step`, `price_tick`, `min_notional_if_known`, `max_notional_if_known`, `allowed_order_types`, `allowed_tif`, `market_mode`, `capability_source`, `identity_status`, `chain_ids`, `contract_or_mint_addresses`, `listing_first_seen`, `research_enabled`, `execution_enabled`, `execution_disabled_reasons`.
- Metadata refresh should replace the complete asset catalogue atomically. A removed asset must not remain eligible through an old cache. Existing holdings remain visible and reconciled even if research or entry eligibility is removed. Never generate a new buy merely because a newly discovered coin is active.
- Deterministic universe tests: inactive/removed/nontradable pair is vetoed; stale metadata is vetoed; an unknown order type/TIF is rejected before transport; invalid/zero/nonfinite increments are vetoed; quantization floors size without exceeding cash/risk caps; below-minimum remainder is reported as dust; a delisted holding remains in inventory; aliases resolve only through stored venue IDs; refreshing a list cannot change the identity of an existing ledger entry; the execution route must match the recorded data venue.

### Gaps
- No authenticated live discovery was performed for this research assignment. The exact paper/live asset catalogue and account-specific restrictions need read-only verification; no listed coin is a current buy recommendation.
- Published Alpaca material is inconsistent about FOK, minimums and some legacy symbols. Do not silently resolve this by choosing the most permissive interpretation.
- Coinbase regional eligibility was not independently verified in this bounded research. Its product interface is a design reference and possible later adapter, not a recommendation to fund an account now.

## Which prices, volume and costs can justify a day or swing trade?

### Takeaway
Use execution-venue bid/ask, tradable depth and actual trade activity to authorize an order; broad-market data supplies context. A candle's midpoint movement is not proof that meaningful quantity traded or that a paper order could execute at that price.

### Cited Findings
- Alpaca's `us` data location is Alpaca US; `us-1` is Kraken US and `eu-1` Kraken EU. Provider/location is meaningful provenance, and the latest orderbook endpoint returns the requested location's bids and asks. — [Latest orderbook](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/reference/cryptolatestorderbooks-1)
- Crypto streaming supplies trades, quotes, orderbooks and bars. Trade messages carry price, size, ID and taker side. Quotes carry bid/ask prices and sizes. Importantly, bars incorporate quote midpoints: zero-trade bars may still have changing OHLC. Late trades can revise earlier minute bars. Book updates replace the size at a price, zero deletes a level, and `r=true` resets to a complete book. — [Real-time crypto data](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/real-time-crypto-pricing-data)
- The historical endpoint supports minute and hour aggregations suitable for 5-minute/15-minute day signals and 1-hour/4-hour swing signals. Page limits cover all requested symbols together, and a next-page token must be checked even if a page is shorter than requested. — [Crypto historical bars](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/reference/cryptobars-1)
- Alpaca confirms crypto bars reflect its own exchange's trades and quotes, including midpoint-priced zero-volume bars. That volume is not total worldwide crypto volume. — [Market data FAQ](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/market-data-faq)
- Alpaca's initial fee tier is 0.15% maker and 0.25% taker per fill. A marketable limit is a taker, not automatically a maker. Fees debit the received currency/asset; CFEE/FEE activities may arrive only at the end of day. — [Crypto fees](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/crypto-fees)
- CoinGecko tickers offer exchange identity, converted volume, spread, source timestamps, stale/anomaly flags and optional 2% depth. These are research context, not an executable Alpaca quote. Its current example has `trust_score: null`; a null quality field must not become a positive guard. — [Coin tickers](https://docs.coingecko.com/reference/coins-id-tickers)
- Alpaca paper fills are quote-based and do not model queue position, market impact, information leakage or latency slippage. Its specification says order quantity is not checked against available quoted size. A paper fill can therefore overstate feasible liquidity. — [Paper trading specification](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/paper-trading)

### Inferences
- Fee hurdle arithmetic, not a return forecast: two 25 bp taker fills require a price ratio above `1 / ((1 - .0025) * (1 - .0025))`, approximately **0.5019%**, before spread, slippage, adverse selection and taxes. Two 15 bp maker fills need about **0.3007%**, but maker eligibility and fills cannot be assumed. Short-horizon hypotheses with 20–40 bp gross expected movement fail the taker cost hurdle even before risk controls.
- `spread_bps = 10000 * (ask-bid)/mid`. Compute buy expected execution by walking asks and sell by walking bids for the proposed quantity. Require enough depth within a configured adverse-price band on both entry and liquidation sides, and cap order size by a conservative fraction of displayed depth and recent actual venue trade notional. Depth may disappear; haircut it and stress its removal.
- Use exact trade-notional sums `sum(price * size)` over a declared rolling window where trades are available. Bar `volume * VWAP` is preferable to `volume * last close` when VWAP is valid; the latter is an approximate observed-dollar-volume metric and should be labelled accordingly. Never use price-only bars to manufacture volume.
- Proposed data record: `source_venue`, `execution_venue`, `pair_id`, `event_at`, `received_at`, `decision_at`, `bid`, `ask`, `bid_qty`, `ask_qty`, `book_snapshot_id`, `book_age_ms`, `trade_age_ms`, `observed_trade_notional_1m/5m/1h/24h`, `volume_coverage`, `midpoint_in_bar`, `bar_revision`, `closed_at`, `fee_profile_at`, `estimated_cost_bps`, `stressed_cost_bps`, `size_supported`, `depth_haircut`, `participation_limit`, `decision_expiry`, `vetoes`.
- Thresholds such as 35 bp maximum crypto spread or $1 million venue daily volume are hypotheses to freeze, stress and calibrate by strategy. They are not universal scientific constants. Do not lower a failed liquidity threshold merely to force a trade. A small Alpaca venue-volume reading can coexist with large worldwide BTC volume without either measurement being erroneous.
- Deterministic microstructure tests: crossed/empty/future/stale quote blocks entries; unknown units or missing opposite-side depth blocks liquidity authorization; huge global volume cannot override low local depth; zero-trade rising-midpoint candle cannot satisfy executed-trade breakout confirmation; late-bar revision cannot rewrite a past decision; incomplete pagination is not full history; disconnected book is invalid until a fresh reset; price-level deletion removes liquidity rather than adds zero-sized orders; price-level replacement cannot be summed as a new level; insufficient depth produces smaller size or abstention; marketable limits incur taker stress fees; uncertain fee tier uses a conservative verified schedule; unresolved late asset fees block new risk but never fabricate a sellable quantity.

### Gaps
- No empirical fee-adjusted day/swing edge, fill probability or slippage distribution has been established by these documents. Need recorded venue quotes/trades/books and frozen out-of-sample experiments.
- Exact crypto `bs`/`as` units are not explicitly defined beyond “bid size” and “ask size” in the current streaming table. The official SDK models pass sizes through without unit normalization. Examples suggest base-asset quantities, but that remains an inference requiring a stronger provider contract or support confirmation before a size-based execution guard can claim verified units. Preserve raw values and `size_unit=unverified`; do not apply equities round-lot conventions. Once units are verified, a fraction of fresh top-of-book size can be a conservative ceiling, but cannot establish deeper-book capacity or guarantee liquidity persists. — [Official SDK Quote model](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/alpacahq/alpaca-py/master/alpaca/data/models/quotes.py); [Official SDK orderbook model](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/alpacahq/alpaca-py/master/alpaca/data/models/orderbooks.py)
- A crypto-specific current data-rate entitlement was not established from the equities subscription table. Do not generalize the IEX 30-symbol equities limit into a crypto limit. Read account responses/rate-limit headers, budget centrally, batch and stream appropriately.
- Not all feeds expose sequence guarantees/checksums. If continuity cannot be proven after reconnect, discard the old book and get a fresh snapshot; do not claim a gap-free history.

## What extra guards apply to meme coins and a market that runs around the clock?

### Takeaway
Meme coins can enter the research universe without receiving an exception to liquidity, identity or execution controls. Around-the-clock operation requires explicit degraded states and recovery; it cannot assume the venue, data or machine stays available continuously.

### Cited Findings
- CoinGecko supports an ID-to-symbol/name mapping with platform and contract addresses; ID and contract mapping is more specific than ticker text alone. — [Coin identity list](https://docs.coingecko.com/reference/coins-list)
- On Solana, a token's mint address is its unique identifier; mint state includes authorities and precision. A token name or logo alone does not identify the token. — [Solana mint documentation](https://solana.com/docs/tokens/basics/create-mint)
- The CFTC documents social-media-driven pump-and-dump schemes in thin/new tokens, including fake news and price spikes; some complete within minutes. It advises against buying from a single social tip or sudden spike and makes no guaranteed-strategy claim. — [CFTC pump-and-dump advisory](https://www.cftc.gov/LearnAndProtect/AdvisoriesAndArticles/beware_virtual_currency_pump_dump.html)
- Alpaca's status page records planned maintenance and API timeouts; “24/7 trading hours” does not mean uninterrupted platform availability. Its customer agreement reserves execution discretion under abnormal conditions and insufficient liquidity. — [Alpaca status](https://status.alpaca.markets/); [Crypto agreement](https://files.alpaca.markets/disclosures/library/Crypto%20Customer%20Agreement.pdf)
- Kraken status distinguishes online, maintenance, cancel-only and post-only states, including periods where cancellation itself is unavailable. Coinbase similarly exposes cancel-only, limit-only, post-only, disabled and auction states; indicative auction books may be crossed and are not normal continuous-market executable quotes. — [Kraken status](https://docs.kraken.com/exchange/api-reference/spot-websocket-v2/status); [Coinbase market states](https://help.coinbase.com/en/coinbase/trading-and-funding/advanced-trade/market-availability); [Coinbase auction mode](https://help.coinbase.com/en/coinbase/trading-and-funding/advanced-trade/auction-mode)

### Inferences
- Resolve news and onchain context through verified project identity, venue asset UUID, chain and contract/mint mappings. A CEX symbol can have several network representations; don't assume each represents a distinct CEX asset, and do not merge a wrapped or bridged token solely by a similar ticker. Require explicit provenance for migrations, renames and redenominations.
- Add structured evidence for token unlocks/vesting, airdrops, exchange listing/delisting, governance/protocol upgrades, exploit/bridge incidents, chain congestion/halt, stablecoin depeg and issuer events. Record source URL, event time, first-seen time, revision time, affected identities, verified versus rumored, and coverage expiry. Absence of a headline means unknown coverage, not proof of no catalyst.
- For meme coins, require a smaller separately capped sleeve, stricter depth/participation and concentration controls, a sufficient history window, identity confidence, and unusual-volume/return flags. These are policy proposals requiring validation. A social hype score can raise scrutiny; it must not disable normal guards or authorize an order by itself. New listing/circuit mode, wildly diverging cross-venue prices, or unsourced viral news should cause abstention or a predefined quarantine interval.
- An account's risk basis is executable value in its quote currency, including stablecoin exposure. A USDT/USDC quote is not silently equivalent to USD during a depeg. A USD-only first implementation avoids some conversion risk but still needs token/issuer-specific guards for stablecoin base assets.
- Deterministic continuity tests: expired heartbeat and socket gap revoke entry eligibility; status maintenance/cancel-only blocks new orders; exchange recovery requires fresh metadata, balances, positions, open orders and market snapshot; a timeout after submit is “unknown” until client-ID lookup proves state, not an automatic duplicate retry; cancellation acknowledgement never proves the order could not fill; partial fill plus cancellation reconciles actual inventory; stop-limit gap can remain unfilled; local stop monitor outage cannot be reported as broker-held protection; restart cannot promote stale news or stale model picks; rate-limit backoff does not starve exit/reconciliation work; storage/disk errors stop new risk while retaining recovery evidence.
- Fee-adjusted size and exit feasibility should be calculated before ticker ranking. “Best trade” means the best eligible candidate under a frozen strategy and data contract at that time; sometimes the correct ranked set is empty. All candidates must retain why-selected and why-rejected evidence for replay.

### Gaps
- This assignment did not verify individual issuer schedules, contract privileges or current coin catalysts. The schema specifies evidence required before a candidate can rely on such a claim.
- Onchain holder concentration can be distorted by exchanges, bridges, liquidity pools and vesting contracts. No universal safe concentration threshold is established here, and it must not be inferred from a raw top-holder percentage.
- Direct DEX execution adds wallet signing, approvals, transfer taxes, sell restrictions, MEV, route/liquidity and transaction-finality failure cases. These were not researched to implementation depth here; exchange-listed meme research does not establish DEX readiness.
