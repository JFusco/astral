# Georgia individual taxes and Alpaca personal Trading API tax constraints

Research date: October 25, 2026. Scope: Georgia resident individual, personal taxable account, ETFs and spot crypto; advisory design constraints rather than preparation of a return. Sources were opened and read. No account settings, lot instructions, purchases, or support requests were made.

## What Georgia tax rules should the bot model?

### Takeaway
Use a tax-year-specific Georgia assumption of 4.99% for 2026 ordinary taxable income, including ordinary investment capital gains absent an applicable exclusion. Do not encode an automatic 2027/2028 reduction from a legislative priorities page; future reductions in the enacted law have conditions. [Georgia DOR, current 2026 updates](https://dor.georgia.gov/taxes/important-tax-updates), [signed HB 463](https://gov.georgia.gov/document/2026-signed-legislation/hb-463/download).

### Cited Findings
- DOR's current “2026 Income Tax Changes” says the rate is a flat 4.99%; the same page lists standard deductions of $15,000 for single/head-of-household/married-separate and $30,000 married-joint. This is a taxable-income rate, not a levy on every gross sale. [DOR updates](https://dor.georgia.gov/taxes/important-tax-updates).
- The Governor's May 11, 2026 announcement confirms HB 463 was signed that day and changes 5.19% to 4.99% beginning January 1, 2026. [Governor announcement](https://gov.georgia.gov/press-releases/2026-05-11/gov-kemp-signs-legislation-lowering-taxes-and-supporting-economic-growth).
- The enacted HB 463 amends O.C.G.A. 48-7-20: 4.99% for 2026; scheduled reductions of 0.125 percentage points annually beginning January 1, 2027 toward 3.99%, subject to delays tied to the standard-deduction triggers in 48-7-27(a)(1.1). Those triggers are checked December 1 and concern the succeeding revenue estimate, prior collections, and reserves. [Signed HB 463, sections 2-1 and 2-3](https://gov.georgia.gov/document/2026-signed-legislation/hb-463/download).
- Georgia's statutory starting point is federal adjusted gross income with state deductions/adjustments. The current individual instructions explicitly tell a full-year filer to enter federal AGI rather than federal taxable income, then apply Georgia adjustments. There is no general preferential long-term capital-gains schedule in the cited flat-rate rule. [Signed HB 463, section 2-3](https://gov.georgia.gov/document/2026-signed-legislation/hb-463/download), [DOR IT-511, page 16, lines 8–10](https://dor.georgia.gov/document/document/2025-it-511-individual-income-tax-booklet/download).
- A conflicting official priorities page says 4.49% for 2027 and 3.99% for 2028. It describes legislative priorities/proposals; those figures differ from the signed law and should not control implementation. [Lieutenant Governor priorities](https://ltgov.georgia.gov/priorities), compared with [signed HB 463](https://gov.georgia.gov/document/2026-signed-legislation/hb-463/download).
- HB 1199 updates the incorporated federal-law date to January 1, 2026, with explicit exceptions; section 4 makes it generally applicable to taxable years beginning on/after January 1, 2026. The law does not mean Georgia automatically adopts every subsequent federal change. [Signed HB 1199, sections 1 and 4](https://gov.georgia.gov/document/2026-signed-legislation/hb-1199/download). The legislature records signature/Act 375 on March 20, 2026. [Bill status](https://www.legis.ga.gov/legislation/72875).
- Georgia's DOR federal-changes page currently opens at a “2024 Changes” section, so it is not a sufficient stand-alone authority for the 2026 conformity state. [DOR federal tax changes](https://dor.georgia.gov/taxes/tax-rules-and-policies/income-tax-federal-tax-changes).
- Certain people aged 62+ or permanently/totally disabled can qualify for a retirement-income exclusion that can include capital gains. This is personal-fact dependent and must not be assumed for this user. [DOR retirement-income exclusion](https://dor.georgia.gov/retirement-income-exclusion).

### Inferences
- For a normal taxable investor account, a 2026 preliminary marginal state estimate may use `estimated_incremental_state_tax = incremental_taxable_gain * 0.0499`, while clearly labeling the estimate and keeping capital-loss netting, exemptions, and federal interactions outside that simple calculation. This follows the flat rate and AGI starting point, but is a design estimate, not a complete tax calculation. [DOR updates](https://dor.georgia.gov/taxes/important-tax-updates), [DOR instructions](https://dor.georgia.gov/document/document/2025-it-511-individual-income-tax-booklet/download).
- Store effective tax year, source date, jurisdiction, and override history. Revalidate the 2027 rate after the December 1 state determination instead of hard-coding an assumed reduction. [Signed HB 463](https://gov.georgia.gov/document/2026-signed-legislation/hb-463/download).

### Gaps
- User's filing status, other income, losses/carryforwards, retirement exclusions, and all external accounts are unknown. A complete combined federal/Georgia tax bill cannot be calculated from the bot account alone.
- No final 2026 individual IT-511 booklet was found in the current DOR booklet index, which lists 2025 as the latest; use enacted 2026 law/current updates for rates and refresh the eventual 2026 instructions. [DOR booklet index](https://dor.georgia.gov/it-511-individual-income-tax-instruction-booklet).

## Can the personal Alpaca Trading API choose tax lots or guarantee wash-sale handling?

### Takeaway
Treat Alpaca FIFO as the documented operating assumption and do not advertise HIFO, LIFO, or specific-identification execution. The strongest current product evidence is a March 8, 2026 response from Alpaca staff plus the personal Trading API schemas; a request-based exception or a later change is unverified. [Alpaca staff response](https://forum.alpaca.markets/t/closing-individual-lots-within-a-profitable-position/18549), [Trading API create-order schema](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/reference/postorder).

### Cited Findings
- In a thread explicitly discussing crypto/equity lots, `Dan_Whitnable_Alpaca` replied March 8, 2026 that Alpaca has no mechanism for lot tracking or specifying which lots to trade; buys/sells and end-of-year gains/losses use FIFO. This is a dated staff forum statement, not an account-specific contract. [Official forum response](https://forum.alpaca.markets/t/closing-individual-lots-within-a-profitable-position/18549).
- The Trading API order schema exposes symbol, side, quantity/notional, order type/time-in-force, execution prices, order classes, client order ID, and other execution fields; it documents no tax-lot selection parameter. A client order ID identifies the order, not the tax lot consumed. [Create an Order](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/reference/postorder), [placing orders](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/orders-at-alpaca).
- The personal-account configuration PATCH schema exposes controls such as fractional trading, margin multiplier, no-short mode, and trade suspension, but no tax-lot disposal-method field. No request was sent. [Account Configurations](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/reference/patchaccountconfig-1).
- The positions API's displayed `avg_entry_price`/`cost_basis` use weighted-average calculations for intraday positions and compressed FIFO for positions from previous days. These displayed position metrics must not be mistaken for a full strict-FIFO tax-lot ledger. [Position Average Entry Price Calculation](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/position-average-entry-price-calculation).
- Alpaca describes a wash sale as a loss sale followed/preceded within 30 days by acquisition of substantially identical securities, including specified IRA purchases. Its support page describes the rule; it does not promise detection across the user's external brokers, spouse accounts, or other strategies. [What is a wash sale?](https://alpaca.markets/support/wash-sale).
- For crypto, IRS 2026 Form 1099-DA instructions require a customer's adequate timely unit identification to be honored where supplied, otherwise generally use earliest units. This is a tax-reporting rule and does not establish that Alpaca implements an identification interface. [IRS 1099-DA instructions, identification of digital assets](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i1099da).

### Inferences
- Maintain immutable fill-level local lots for analytical reconciliation, with FIFO marked as the broker assumption and the broker's tax statements authoritative for reconciliation. Do not label hypothetical local HIFO calculations as actual broker disposal instructions or a valid election. [Alpaca staff response](https://forum.alpaca.markets/t/closing-individual-lots-within-a-profitable-position/18549), [order schema](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/reference/postorder).
- Put wash-sale monitoring above individual strategy workers: a swing worker's loss exit and an intraday worker's replacement buy can interact in one taxable account. External-account coverage must display incomplete when those feeds are absent. This is a product-control inference from the securities rule; it is not a broker guarantee. [Alpaca wash-sale description](https://alpaca.markets/support/wash-sale).

### Gaps
- No official personal Trading API feature was found for requesting HIFO/specific ID via order, configuration, or MCP. No evidence establishes a manual request path, cutoff, or settlement deadline; do not claim an email/support request will change matching. Account-specific broker confirmation would be needed before relying on an exception.
- Do not reuse Broker API partner tax-document/lot capabilities as proof of availability for this personal account. The verified execution/configuration sources above are `/v2` personal Trading API.
- A displayed average entry-price change is not enough evidence of actual tax lot selection. Annual/corrected broker statements must be reconciled before estimated tax reports are treated as complete.

## What activity, fee, and tax documents should the paper/live ledgers retain?

### Takeaway
Paper fills are simulated rather than real taxable dispositions. Build a separate hypothetical tax ledger for stress tests now, while preserving all actual fills/fees/adjustments for a future live account. [Alpaca paper trading specification](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/paper-trading).

### Cited Findings
- Alpaca paper trading uses simulated execution without routing an order to a live exchange and does not use real money. Its paper assumptions exclude dividends and regulatory fees, among other real-world differences. Thus a paper tax simulation must add relevant scenarios rather than claiming broker paper P&L already models taxes. [Paper Trading](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/paper-trading).
- Alpaca's April 2026 support page says US customers with reportable activity receive a 1099 statement including 1099-B and dividend/interest-related forms. This does not independently verify its crypto 1099-DA delivery details. [Which tax documents are provided?](https://alpaca.markets/support/tax-documents-info).
- The personal Trading API `/v2/account/activities` supplies paginated activity history and can filter by order ID. Its creation-date filter differs from settlement time; nontrade fees are typically created the following day in UTC. [Retrieve Account Activities](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/reference/getaccountactivities-2).
- Activity objects include fill IDs/timestamps/quantity/price and nontrade net amounts. Published types include `CFEE` crypto fees, `FEE` USD fees, dividends, capital-gain distributions, transfers, splits, and reorganizations. [Account Activities](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/account-activities).
- IRS requires reporting taxable digital-asset income/gains/losses even without a 1099-DA; proceeds are not profit. Exchanges for dollars, other assets, and crypto used to pay broker costs can be dispositions. [Understanding your Form 1099-DA](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/understanding-your-form-1099-da).
- For 2026, brokers generally must report crypto proceeds and basis for covered digital assets. Covered status generally requires acquisition after 2025 in that broker's custodial account and continued holding there through sale; older or transferred-in assets are noncovered for this reporting definition. This reporting label does not mean all crypto is a security for wash-sale purposes. [IRS 2026 Form 1099-DA instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i1099da).

### Inferences
- Ingest/persist activities with deduplication by activity ID, overlap polling for delayed fees/corrections, retain trade/created/settlement timestamps separately, and reconcile cash, inventory, gross proceeds, fees, basis, dividends, and corporate actions. Pending/unmatched items remain visible; they must not silently become zero fees/taxes. [Activity API](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/reference/getaccountactivities-2), [activity types](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/account-activities).
- Stress-test lots spanning tax-year boundaries, multiple same-ticker strategies, partial fills, fee-paid-in-crypto inventory reductions, transferred-in lots with missing basis, duplicate/delayed fee postings, splits and dividends, and corrected tax statements. These are proposed deterministic scenarios based on the activity/reporting distinctions above, not claims that every scenario is simulated by Alpaca. [Activity types](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/account-activities), [IRS reporting guidance](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/understanding-your-form-1099-da).
- Display pre-tax and estimated after-tax results side by side. Tax estimation may help compare otherwise acceptable discretionary trades; it must never veto a required protective exit solely to defer tax. This is the proposed control policy honoring the user's profit/risk objective, not a tax-law requirement.

### Gaps
- An Alpaca-specific current 1099-DA delivery/support page was not found despite targeted searches. IRS duties are verified, but actual Alpaca crypto tax statement fields, availability, and correction process for this account remain unverified; do not promise a form/date or full basis coverage.
- The bot has not yet established complete activity imports or external-account basis histories in this research task. A fill's price plus displayed position cost basis cannot certify final tax reporting or exact net profit.
