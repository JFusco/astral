# Size trades while preserving after-tax wealth

**ASTRAL should size each position from verified capital and stressed loss, then use tax estimates to compare otherwise permissible discretionary trades. Protective exits must retain priority.** The objective is greater expected wealth after trading costs and taxes, rather than the smallest tax bill or the largest number of trades. Day trades and short swings generally generate short-term results, so stretching a losing trade toward a favorable holding period conflicts with the strategy. Paper tax estimates should remain a separate, explicitly hypothetical ledger. The current engine reconciles fills, fee activities, inventory, and strategy ownership; it does **not** implement a complete tax-lot adapter or a personal tax calculation. This report assumes an individual taxable account in Georgia and uses research checked **October 25, 2026**; filing status, outside holdings, carryforwards, and elections remain unknown.

## Size from loss and capital before considering tax

The numerical planner should explain the binding constraint on every proposed purchase. Its starting relationship is `quantity = planned_loss_budget / stressed_loss_per_unit`, followed by cash, exposure, venue minimums, increments, and execution constraints. Stressed loss includes the distance from entry to the planning stop, fees, slippage, and an explicit adverse-gap allowance. Round quantity down and recompute every constraint. The principle connects stop distance and available risk capital; the chosen risk percentage is an experiment policy, not a scientifically established optimal amount. ([CME position sizing](https://www.cmegroup.com/education/courses/trade-and-risk-management/proper-position-size))

ASTRAL's current planner uses **0.25% of equity for swing proposals and 0.10% for intraday proposals**. A planning stop does not establish an enforceable loss ceiling. Because unattended protective execution remains unvalidated, the current executor additionally treats positions and pending purchases at their full value: the shared unprotected allowance is at most **0.5% of equity**, with separate 0.25% swing and 0.10% intraday allowances and a cost reserve. Existing commitments reduce available capacity. These are implemented conservative constraints, not permissions to allocate the entire allowance repeatedly. Alpaca strategy execution remains shadow-only while protection and evidence requirements are unresolved. The [current engine](../backend/engine.py) and [numerical planner](../backend/risk.py) define these separate contracts.

For example, a hypothetical $10,000 account has a $25 swing planned-loss budget. A $2.20 stressed loss per unit initially permits 11 units after rounding. But the unprotected swing allowance, cost reserve, and order/cash limits can reduce the permitted purchase much further. The explanation must show that reduction. An agent's confidence score cannot increase the hard allowance. Estimated taxes on realized gains can eventually inform a separate cash reserve, but that reserve is proposed bookkeeping rather than money Alpaca has withheld.

## Day and swing gains require annual tax context

For an ordinary investor, an asset held **one year or less** generally produces short-term capital results; long-term treatment generally requires more than one year. Net short-term gains enter ordinary income treatment. Annual netting and loss limitations matter: excess net capital losses generally offset at most $3,000 of other income, or $1,500 for married filing separately, with remaining losses carried forward. A profitable exit therefore cannot be assigned an accurate personal tax cost simply by multiplying its gain by a generic rate. ([IRS Topic 409](https://www.irs.gov/taxtopics/tc409))

Some taxpayers also owe the **3.8% net investment income tax**, determined from investment income and income above the applicable filing-status threshold. Georgia's published **2026 flat income-tax rate is 4.99%**; that is a taxable-income rate, not a charge on gross sales. Its enacted future reductions contain conditions, so ASTRAL should revalidate the 2027 rate after the December determination instead of copying proposed rates from a policy announcement. Store tax year, source date, assumptions, and effective dates with each estimate. ([IRS NIIT](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/net-investment-income-tax), [Georgia DOR](https://dor.georgia.gov/taxes/important-tax-updates), [signed HB 463](https://gov.georgia.gov/document/2026-signed-legislation/hb-463/download))

A proposed advisor should calculate the incremental difference between annual scenarios with and without a discretionary action. It should include configured short-term and long-term assumptions, known losses, applicable state adjustments, and explicit uncertainty. **An unknown tax profile produces an unavailable estimate, never an assumed zero rate.** It can compare holding, selling, or another eligible entry after costs and expected opportunity loss. It cannot postpone a protective sell, conceal a loss circuit, or spend a forecast tax benefit as guaranteed cash.

Continuous trading does not automatically establish trader tax status. The IRS applies substantive activity and continuity tests; a section 475(f) election has timing and scope requirements. ASTRAL should default to investor accounting and record any professionally established election with its effective date, rather than infer one from trade count. Securities and commodities provisions also differ, so a securities election cannot silently cover every crypto instrument. ([IRS Topic 429](https://www.irs.gov/taxtopics/tc429), [26 USC 475](https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/119-118/t26/s475/))

## Wash-sale awareness must span strategies and accounts

Section 1091 covers loss sales involving substantially identical stock or securities acquired within **30 days before through 30 days after** the sale. This is a 61-day observation window, not merely a block on tomorrow's repurchase. An intraday purchase can interact with a swing loss in the same account. Relevant outside-account, spouse, and retirement-account activity can also matter; an IRA replacement has different basis consequences. Missing feeds must produce an incomplete-scope warning rather than a claim that the broker's reported wash sales capture the taxpayer's entire situation. ([26 USC 1091](https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/119-118/t26/s1091/), [IRS Publication 550](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p550))

**Do not encode “all crypto is wash-sale exempt.”** The statutory stock/security scope requires an instrument classification. Ordinary direct spot crypto does not automatically enter that scope merely because it is digital, while tokenized securities provide a concrete exception: the 2026 Form 1099-DA instructions explicitly discuss their wash-sale reporting. Similarly, two ETF tickers are not automatically safe substitutes. Keep classification, source, review date, effective period, and unresolved status as metadata; model prose cannot establish a legal classification. ([IRS 2026 Form 1099-DA instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i1099da))

A wash sale generally concerns loss recognition and basis adjustments, rather than a categorical ban on a potentially profitable trade. The proposed overlay should disclose estimated deferral and incomplete coverage, preserve replacement-unit relationships, and keep forward-window outcomes provisional. A risk-reducing sale remains available regardless of the advisory result. ETF structures also require review: precious-metal trusts, commodity products, partnerships, and futures-based funds cannot all inherit an ordinary stock-fund tax template. ([IRS Publication 550](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p550))

## Lot identity and delayed fees prevent false profits

Alpaca's personal Trading API documents no tax-lot selection field. In a **March 8, 2026** forum response, Alpaca staff stated that customers cannot specify lots and that gains/losses use FIFO. Treat this as dated product evidence requiring refresh, rather than an account-specific contractual guarantee. A local “HIFO” calculation does not instruct Alpaca to dispose of those units. Displayed average entry price is also unsuitable as a tax ledger: Alpaca uses weighted-average intraday calculations and compressed FIFO for older positions. ([Alpaca staff response](https://forum.alpaca.markets/t/closing-individual-lots-within-a-profitable-position/18549), [order schema](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/reference/postorder), [position calculations](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/position-average-entry-price-calculation))

There is an important temporary distinction for custodial crypto. **IRS Notice 2026-20 extends identification relief through December 31, 2026**, allowing qualifying contemporaneous taxpayer records or standing instructions under its conditions. It does not change broker information reporting, so taxpayer and broker calculations can differ. This is neither an implemented ASTRAL election nor proof of Alpaca lot-selection support. Before January 1, 2027, recheck authoritative guidance and the identification mechanism; absent a further change, ordinary broker-communication requirements return. Never reconstruct advantageous lot choices after seeing the sale outcome. ([IRS Notice 2026-20](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-drop/n-26-20.pdf))

The proposed ledger must retain account/wallet, asset identity, acquisition and disposal times, exact gross/net units, USD valuation source, fee currency and quantity, adjusted basis, remaining units, transfer lineage, selected-lot records, and rule version. Crypto-to-crypto exchanges can be dispositions, and paying transaction services with crypto can create a separate fee-unit disposition. Unknown transferred basis must remain unknown. Account-specific basis rules prohibit an unrestricted global highest-cost pool. ([IRS digital-asset FAQs](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/international-taxpayers/frequently-asked-questions-on-digital-asset-transactions), [IRS Rev. Proc. 2024-28](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-drop/rp-24-28.pdf))

Current fill/fee inventory reconciliation is a foundation for this ledger, not its completion. Alpaca activities can arrive later, and creation-date filters differ from settlement timing. Import overlapping windows, deduplicate by activity ID, preserve corrections, and reconcile quantities as well as dollars. A fee deducted in received crypto changes saleable inventory. Unmatched fee or quantity differences must remain visible; they cannot be labeled dust and erased to improve P&L. Tax statements and local records need comparison rather than automatic replacement. ([Alpaca activities API](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/reference/getaccountactivities-2), [activity types](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/account-activities))

## Conclusion

The next useful tax milestone is a **hypothetical, versioned advisor tested against fixed scenarios**, with no trading authority. Replay partial lots, delayed and duplicate fees, missing transfer basis, cross-strategy replacements, IRA purchases, one-year/leap-year boundaries, corrected statements, and the 2026/2027 identification transition. A required protective exit should yield the same executable decision with tax awareness enabled or disabled. Paper fills simulate activity without real market routing; their tax calculations are scenario analysis, not realized taxable gains. ([Alpaca paper trading](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/paper-trading))

That separation makes tax efficiency measurable without allowing it to weaken safety. Compare frozen strategies on net pretax wealth, hypothetical after-tax wealth, turnover, drawdown, and missed opportunities under the same market snapshots and cost assumptions. If an apparent tax improvement depends on unknown outside losses, unavailable lot instructions, or keeping an unsafe position open, the evidence does not support the improvement. ASTRAL can explain those dependencies deterministically while leaving personal tax treatment and elections to an explicitly configured, professionally reviewed profile.
