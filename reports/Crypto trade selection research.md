# Demand executable evidence before trading crypto

**ASTRAL should search a broad crypto universe, then require venue liquidity, timely evidence, a frozen trading signal and independently calculated size before considering an order.** This applies equally to Bitcoin, smaller tokens and meme coins; none receives a spread, identity or risk exception. Day and swing strategies require separate datasets, holding rules and evaluation, while sharing account capital and exposure limits. Historical crypto momentum research motivates experiments but does not establish a profitable five-minute strategy. At Alpaca's initial fee tier, two taker fills require approximately **0.502% price appreciation just to cover fees**, before spread and slippage. Current ASTRAL discovery and shadow controls expose missing evidence; they do not yet implement the proposed intraday data, crypto event calendar, verified token identity or depth-based execution pipeline. The immediate objective is a reproducible opportunity laboratory that can explain both a selection and an abstention, then demonstrate whether either deterministic rules or an added AI reviewer improves unseen, cost-adjusted results. Research checked **October 25, 2026**. ([Historical research](https://www.nber.org/papers/w24877), [Alpaca fee schedule](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/crypto-fees))

## Broad discovery must narrow into account-specific eligibility

Alpaca is the practical first venue because ASTRAL already has its paper integration and Georgia appears in its supported US jurisdictions. Its published catalogue includes DOGE, SHIB, PEPE, BONK and other meme-associated pairs, alongside major tokens. **A documented listing is neither an account-specific permission nor a buy signal.** Query active crypto assets, preserve asset UUIDs and increments, and independently verify tradability, account permissions, allowed order combinations and freshness. Alpaca crypto is spot, non-marginable and unavailable for short selling; the initial strategy set should therefore remain cash-funded and long-only. ([Georgia availability](https://alpaca.markets/support/alpaca-cryptocurrency), [Supported pairs](https://alpaca.markets/support/what-cryptocurrencies-does-alpaca-currently-support), [Crypto trading contract](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/crypto-trading))

Each candidate needs a versioned identity record: execution venue, asset UUID, canonical pair, base/quote identity, applicable chain and contract or native-asset mapping, increments, minimums, status and metadata receipt time. Symbol text alone cannot join news, external prices and holdings. Solana explicitly identifies a token by its mint address; CoinGecko provides platform/contract mapping for cross-checks. Wrapped assets, migrations and colliding tickers require explicit mappings with effective dates. Current ASTRAL has **read-only discovery of Alpaca's active crypto catalogue**, while its existing eight execution instruments remain fixed. External chain/contract identity adapters and meme-specific metadata are not implemented. Discovery must not silently enable execution. ([Solana identity](https://solana.com/docs/tokens/basics/create-mint), [CoinGecko identity mapping](https://docs.coingecko.com/reference/coins-list))

Keep later Coinbase and Kraken adapters separate. Kraken publishes pair status, tick size and minimums, and Georgia is absent from its current state exclusions, but account and asset restrictions still require confirmation. Coinbase exposes cancel-only, limit-only, disabled and auction modes; its Advanced sandbox uses static mocked responses and cannot validate live matching. Additional venues add independent balances, fee schedules, order state and reconciliation. Alpaca's order documentation itself conflicts about FOK support; use verified GTC/IOC capabilities rather than the most permissive document. Do not assume crypto brackets, OCO, trailing stops or post-only orders exist. ([Kraken pairs](https://docs.kraken.com/api-reference/market-data/get-tradable-asset-pairs), [Kraken jurisdictions](https://support.kraken.com/articles/where-is-kraken-licensed-or-regulated), [Coinbase product states](https://docs.cdp.coinbase.com/api-reference/advanced-trade-api/rest-api/products/get-product), [Coinbase sandbox](https://docs.cdp.coinbase.com/coinbase-app/advanced-trade-apis/sandbox), [Alpaca capabilities](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/crypto-orders))

## Executable liquidity must precede signal strength and size

Authorize liquidity from the execution venue's fresh bid, ask, actual trades and verified book, while treating aggregate market data as context. Alpaca labels `us`, `us-1` and `eu-1` as different data locations. Its crypto candles incorporate quote midpoints, so OHLC can change without executed trades; late trades can also revise earlier minute bars. **A rising midpoint candle cannot prove a trade-confirmed breakout, and Alpaca venue volume is not worldwide coin volume.** Current ASTRAL vetoes crypto trade-breakout authorization from midpoint-based OHLC and vetoes size authorization when quote-size units remain unverified. These restrictions prevent false certainty; a trade-derived aggregation and verified liquidity adapter are still required. ([Crypto stream semantics](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/real-time-crypto-pricing-data), [Orderbook locations](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/reference/cryptolatestorderbooks-1), [Market data FAQ](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/market-data-faq))

Compute spread as `10,000 × (ask − bid) / midpoint`. Proposed execution checks should walk asks for entry and bids for liquidation, haircut displayed size and cap participation in recent actual traded notional. Missing depth cannot become invented capacity. Initial research settings to freeze and test include quotes no older than two seconds for day trades or five seconds for swings, $1 million observed venue turnover over 24 hours, participation below 0.1% of trailing-minute notional, and size below 5% of verified depth inside the price collar. These are **experimental policy settings, not established safe thresholds**. The app's existing 15-second quote and daily-volume screens are earlier operational checks; they do not satisfy this proposed contract.

Alpaca initially charges 15 basis points maker and 25 taker per fill. A marketable limit is a taker. Two taker fees imply a break-even price ratio above `1 / (0.9975 × 0.9975)`, approximately 1.005019. Planning should conservatively assume taker execution unless a measured queue-aware model supports another assumption. Fees arrive in the received asset and fee activities can post later, so apparent sellable quantity and P&L require reconciliation. Marketable limits bound price while accepting nonexecution; passive limits do not guarantee fills or maker economics. Stop-limits can remain unfilled after a gap. ([Fees and posting](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/crypto-fees), [Order support](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/crypto-orders))

Sizing should take the minimum of loss-budget quantity, affordable cash including fees, remaining full-value exposure budget, executable liquidity capacity and symbol/group headroom, then floor to the venue increment and recheck minimums. Record the binding cap, planned loss, stressed loss and invalidation. Current ASTRAL uses ATR-based planning but retains **full-value unprotected exposure caps of 0.25% of equity for swing, 0.10% for intraday and 0.50% shared**, including pending risk. A local conditional monitor does not make maximum loss equal to stop distance. More adverse cost or a wider stop must never increase quantity. Future liquidity and gap calibration should tighten this calculation rather than substitute an AI confidence score.

## Day and swing hypotheses need different clocks and catalysts

Published research finds historical cryptocurrency momentum and attention effects, while newer factor research uses weekly portfolios and explicitly retains inactive coins. Its five-minute observations document jumps; they do not establish a five-minute momentum edge. Start with a small, registered experiment set rather than an indicator search that repeatedly selects its own winner. **The current daily-bar research feed cannot validate 5m, 15m or 4h hypotheses.** Alpaca supports minute/hour historical aggregation, but coverage, pagination, bar provenance and first-available revisions still need explicit handling. ([Momentum research](https://www.nber.org/papers/w24877), [Weekly factors and jumps](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.14435v4), [Historical bar interface](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/reference/cryptobars-1))

| Proposed experiment | Entry evidence | Exit hypothesis and evaluation boundary |
|---|---|---|
| 15m day breakout; 5m separate challenger | Completed trade-derived breakout, volume confirmation, 1h regime, fresh executable quote | ATR/structural invalidation or four-hour timeout; compare each timeframe independently |
| 15m pullback continuation | Positive 1h trend, predefined pullback and completed recovery | Trend invalidation, adverse boundary or four-hour timeout |
| 5m/15m range challenger | Predeclared low-trend regime; no jump, launch or critical-event state | Mean touch, adverse boundary or one-hour timeout |
| 4h swing continuation | Completed breakout or separately registered pullback, 1d context and full warmup | ATR/trend exit or two-to-five-day holding limit |
| Catalyst observation | Verified event identity, first-seen time and release version | Observation only until independently validated |

News and events should modify explicit rules, not issue discretionary orders. Required crypto coverage includes protocol/security incidents, unlocks and vesting, listings/delistings, maintenance and selected macro releases. Crypto should not inherit a corporate earnings calendar literally. Preserve source, publication, receipt, revision, affected identities, event interval, date precision and coverage expiry. A roadmap saying Q4 must remain an interval; an unlock schedule does not demonstrate a beneficiary claimed, deposited or sold tokens. Arbitrum's supply documentation illustrates both multiple vesting mechanisms and dated supply figures. The Federal Reserve supplies a direct macro calendar, but no directional coin prediction. ([Arbitrum supply](https://docs.arbitrum.foundation/token-supply), [Vesting implementation](https://github.com/ArbitrumFoundation/governance/blob/main/src/ArbitrumVestingWallet.sol), [FOMC calendar](https://www.federalreserve.gov/monetarypolicy/fomccalendars.htm))

Current ASTRAL ingests news metadata but lacks the required upcoming-event calendar. Unknown coverage therefore prevents qualified entries rather than becoming “no news.” For meme coins, require adequate history, verified identity, credible exit liquidity and tighter separately capped exposure; add administrative powers and concentration evidence where the strategy requires them. Exchange, pool and vesting wallets complicate raw holder-concentration numbers. Social attention can raise scrutiny, never waive guards. CFTC warnings document thin-token pump-and-dump patterns; deterministic anomaly filters cannot guarantee manipulation detection. Direct DEX execution remains outside current capability because wallet signing, approvals, sell restrictions, MEV and finality introduce additional unimplemented controls. ([CFTC advisory](https://www.cftc.gov/LearnAndProtect/AdvisoriesAndArticles/beware_virtual_currency_pump_dump.html))

## Forward evidence must defeat costs, leakage and AI storytelling

The selection pipeline should persist the full candidate universe, eligibility vetoes, frozen numerical signal, ranking, size, timing decision, execution outcome and subsequent marks. Rank only eligible candidates. Until unseen data supplies a defensible expectancy estimate, show signal strength and cost hurdle separately; do not invent expected return from target distance. ASTRAL's tool-disabled **GPT-6.1 Sol/high reviewer** explains supplied evidence and counterarguments under schema validation, with four attempts per UTC day per database. It cannot place orders or override vetoes. CLI subscription usage shares account limits and has unknown attributable USD; it is not a free unlimited worker.

Register every strategy, timeframe, parameter and prompt variant before evaluation. Use chronological walk-forward tests, purge overlapping outcome intervals, preserve delisted assets, keep an untouched final window and then collect prospective shadow/paper observations. Ordinary time-series splitting preserves chronology but does not automatically purge variable holding periods. Multiple-search diagnostics such as Deflated Sharpe Ratio and probability of backtest overfitting help assess selection bias only when failed trials remain visible. A current LLM can contain knowledge of historical outcomes despite browsing being disabled; prospective evidence is the cleaner AI comparison. ([Time-series splitting](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/generated/sklearn.model_selection.TimeSeriesSplit.html), [Deflated Sharpe Ratio](https://www.davidhbailey.com/dhbpapers/deflated-sharpe.pdf), [Backtest overfitting](https://carmamaths.org/resources/jon/backtest2.pdf))

| Injected edge case | Required deterministic observable result |
|---|---|
| Ticker collision, migration or unknown size units | Quarantine candidate with conflicting IDs or missing contract named |
| Future news, late revision or unfinished candle | Exclude unavailable evidence; preserve original decision and version |
| Zero-trade midpoint rise or global-volume substitution | Reject trade-confirmed breakout/liquidity claim |
| Feed gap, crossed quote, deleted depth or maintenance | Pause affected entries; require fresh snapshot and reconciled state |
| Gap through stop; stop and target within one OHLC bar | Use later executable quotes or disclose ambiguous outcome; never assume favorable ordering |
| Timeout after submit, partial fill during cancel, restart | Recover original client ID and actual fills; retain reservation until resolved |
| Concurrent sleeves selling the same coin | Reserve sleeve inventory and prevent cross-sleeve liquidation |
| Late asset fee, dust or truncated history page | Deduplicate/reconcile; expose mismatch or incomplete coverage |
| Hallucinated citation, changed fact or malicious article instruction | Reject model output; no new execution authority |

These are acceptance requirements, **not a claim that every case is implemented**. Current shadow timing, inventory reconciliation, immutable decision contexts and synthetic timing replay supply an initial test surface. Provider paper also omits queue priority, impact and latency slippage, and does not constrain fills to displayed quote size. Compare against cash, deterministic same-signal/no-LLM selection, liquidity-first selection and BTC-only rules under identical costs and capital. Report net expectancy, attempted versus filled orders, drawdown, tails, turnover, latency and uncertainty by regime. Stress taker fees, wider spreads, delayed fills, disappearing liquidity and outages. No universal trade count proves an edge. ([Paper limitations](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/paper-trading))

## Conclusion

The $100–$300 monthly budget favors one venue, shared data capture and infrequent bounded reviews before paid breadth. Spend first on verifiable execution data and recoverable state; expand news or alternate venues only when a measured coverage gap justifies the expense. A local computer does not establish continuous hosted availability, and a 24/7 market does not guarantee venue uptime.

The next meaningful promotion is a frozen 15m or 4h candidate with verified identity, trade-derived history, event coverage and feasible exit liquidity, observed prospectively alongside its no-LLM baseline. An empty eligible set is a valid measurement. Broadening discovery should increase the opportunity to find a defensible trade, while preserving the ability to explain why every tempting alternative was rejected.
