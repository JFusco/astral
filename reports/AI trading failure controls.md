# Make AI trading failures testable deterministically

**ASTRAL can deterministically prevent many false claims, invalid decisions, and execution errors; it cannot deterministically guarantee profitable ticker selection or timing.** Comparable public systems expose recurring weaknesses in historical data, simulated fills, accounting, model reasoning, and learning evaluation. Those are useful engineering evidence, independent of whether their authors used AI to write code. The strongest design is a bounded research model, a separate numerical decision and risk authority, and one recoverable executor. For crypto day and swing trading, fees, executable liquidity, instrument identity, and decision-time evidence deserve the same scrutiny as the signal. This research was checked **October 25, 2026** and distinguishes author-described limitations, acknowledged historical bugs, and reported benchmark findings. ASTRAL's current working tree contains several corresponding guards and adversarial fixtures, but strategy execution remains paper-shadow-only and no positive trading edge has been established.

## Public systems expose three different acceptance questions

Ask whether a system implements its policy correctly, whether its evaluation uses honest information and execution assumptions, and whether its frozen policy earns a net advantage. These are separate acceptance questions. An executable backtester can calculate the wrong inventory; an honest backtest can reveal an unprofitable strategy; a profitable historical strategy can fail after its regime changes. **Agent agreement and a convincing explanation do not answer any of these questions by themselves.** This is an engineering synthesis of the source evidence below, rather than a result measured on ASTRAL.

| System or study | What the primary evidence establishes | Consequence for ASTRAL |
|---|---|---|
| **AI Hedge Fund** | The authors describe an educational proof of concept without actual trading; its roadmap separates simulated funds from future broker and scheduler work. | A research interface or paper NAV cannot establish unattended broker operation. ([Repository](https://github.com/virattt/ai-hedge-fund), [roadmap](https://github.com/virattt/ai-hedge-fund/blob/main/ROADMAP.md)) |
| **TradingAgents** | Maintainers document corrected data-timing, learning-reflection, missing-input, and reasoning defects across releases. | Turn each documented failure into a regression contract; do not describe fixed defects as current vulnerabilities. ([Changelog](https://github.com/TauricResearch/TradingAgents/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md)) |
| **Freqtrade/FreqAI** | Candle backtests have explicit fill assumptions; reinforcement-learning documentation warns that agents can exploit an artificial environment. | Reproduce assumptions explicitly and test deviations before trusting simulated returns. ([Backtesting](https://www.freqtrade.io/en/stable/backtesting/#assumptions-made-by-backtesting), [RL guidance](https://www.freqtrade.io/en/stable/freqai-reinforcement-learning/)) |
| **Market-Bench** | Generated backtesters can execute successfully while diverging from reference capital, inventory, timing, spread, or P&L. | Passing syntax and happy-path tests is insufficient; assert financial state transitions against independent fixtures. ([Paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.12264v2)) |
| **KTD-Fin** | In its blinded CSI300 experiment, authors report negative factor-attributed selection alpha for nine of ten agents and approximately neutral alpha for one. | Separate market/style exposure from selection skill. Its A-share inputs do not establish the same result for crypto. ([Paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.28359v1)) |
| **Quant-Arena** | Authors report no tested model beating the equal-weight market in a sealed 2024 A-share evaluation after research on 2022–2023. | Freeze rules before unseen evaluation. This recent preprint uses a gross market reference against cost-bearing strategies. ([Paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.30113v1)) |

These projects were not independently installed and exhaustively audited in this research. No reliable primary evidence established that any named repository was “vibe coded.” Nof1/Alpha Arena is relevant, but its technical post was inaccessible during collection; leaderboard anecdotes were excluded. The useful comparison is therefore specific contracts and evidence, rather than a blanket claim that similar bots do not work.

## Historical leakage can make the wrong trade look intelligent

TradingAgents issue **#1251** documents lessons resolved from later outcomes being recalled into earlier dated decisions. A lesson's ticker and relevance were insufficient: its resolution time also mattered. The maintainer changelog records a correction. ASTRAL should require `resolved_at <= decision_at` for any future learning retrieval and preserve the inputs that actually existed then. This learning control is proposed; storing research packets does not implement it. ([Issue #1251](https://github.com/TauricResearch/TradingAgents/issues/1251), [fix record](https://github.com/TauricResearch/TradingAgents/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md))

Issue **#1518** documents another timing mismatch: a decision could read news through a day's UTC boundary while its performance started from that day's US close. After-close information could consequently improve an entry priced before that information existed. The maintainers record a correction and warn about mixing older settled logs with newer scoring conventions. ASTRAL should preserve publication, revision, first observation, decision completion, order arrival, and fill times separately. A published-yesterday article first received today was unavailable to yesterday's bot. ([Issue #1518](https://github.com/TauricResearch/TradingAgents/issues/1518), [migration note](https://github.com/TauricResearch/TradingAgents/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md))

A useful deterministic property is **prefix invariance**: appending arbitrary future observations must not alter an earlier decision, its eligible sources, or its input hash. Apply it to candles, calendars, token listings, delistings, news revisions, feature scaling, and eventually learned lessons. Exercise every signal branch; Freqtrade warns that its lookahead analysis cannot verify a signal that never triggers. Today’s surviving coin list also cannot become the historical tradable universe. ([Lookahead analysis](https://www.freqtrade.io/en/stable/lookahead-analysis/))

## Adversarial invariants turn failure stories into acceptance tests

The following matrix is an **ASTRAL engineering acceptance plan**, inferred from the preceding evidence and current source inspection. P0 addresses order authority, account correctness, and invalid evidence; P1 addresses evaluation and operational completeness. “Present” means the working tree contains a guard and relevant fixture, not independent certification or a promise about every broker failure.

| Priority and failure | Deterministic invariant | Adversarial proof and current boundary |
|---|---|---|
| **P0 — invented or duplicated picks** | Picks use eligible canonical symbols and existing evidence IDs; code owns spread, volume, and notional. | Invent a coin/citation, duplicate a pick, alter sizing, or propose a vetoed packet: reject the review. Present in `research.py` and selection/worker tests. |
| **P0 — stale or revised inputs** | Every required observation is available by decision time and remains inside its freshness/coverage contract. | Future history receipt, stale quote, tomorrow's news revision, missing calendar: veto. Present fixtures; complete point-in-time calendar adapters remain absent. |
| **P0 — ambiguous crypto prices or depth** | The strategy accepts only verified bar semantics and normalized quote-size units. | Supply quote-midpoint OHLC, zero depth, or unknown size units: veto. Present. The current trade-based breakout cannot simply interpret mixed bars as executed trades. |
| **P0 — duplicate orders after timeout** | Persist intent and stable identity first; unknown status retains reservations and enters reconciliation. | Broker accepts then drops response; restart; later readback returns an error: no second submission or premature capital release. Present lifecycle fixtures; broaden connected fault evidence separately. |
| **P0 — shared capital or inventory spent twice** | Account exposure and pending orders constrain all strategies; a sell consumes only its own unreserved sleeve. | Concurrent capital demands, an existing intraday sell plus another sell, or an external order: cap or veto. Present shared-budget and sibling-sell fixtures. |
| **P0 — late fees silently alter ownership** | Reconcile credited quantities, fee currency, and broker positions; ambiguous allocation is review-required. | Replay a fee twice, deliver it late, or make ownership ambiguous: deduplicate or block affected strategy decisions. Present inventory fixtures; unresolved differences remain visible. |
| **P0 — untrusted text gains authority** | Research has no broker credentials or execution tool; its output cannot change limits or permission flags. | Headline orders a market buy or secret disclosure; worker emits a tool event: accept no actionable review. Environment/command/output tests are present; ranking manipulation still needs evaluation. |
| **P0 — symbol discovery becomes permission** | Venue listing, research readiness, and execution allowance are separate states. | A valid DOGE/USD metadata fixture remains discovery-only; duplicate IDs, bad increments, or disabled trading invalidate eligibility. Present. DEX contract risks need a dedicated adapter. |
| **P1 — same-bar or impossible fills** | Decisions precede executable fills; expired entry authority is rechecked after latency. | Price gaps through a stop or permission expires during inference: no fill at the earlier signal/stop price. Present synthetic replay; depth, queue, and partial-fill simulation remain future work. |
| **P1 — candle backtest overstates net return** | Fee/slippage assumptions are explicit; increasing costs cannot improve an identical round trip. | Stress maker-to-taker changes, spread expansion, partial IOC, and vanished depth. Cost monotonicity is present; realistic fill probability is unvalidated. |
| **P1 — learning fits noise or simulator tricks** | Register every trial, freeze policy, seal evaluation data, and prohibit self-promotion. | Retrieve a future lesson, tune against holdout, or exploit stale marks: deny promotion. A real learning and promotion ledger remains proposed. |
| **P1 — a stopped process appears alive** | Recovery reconciles durable state before entries; independent monitoring detects process/feed failure. | Kill at persistence/submission boundaries; expire credentials; exhaust quota/disk; jump the clock. Some restart/unknown-order fixtures exist; hosted watchdog and observed 24/7 recovery remain unproven. |

Broker evidence strengthens these contracts: Alpaca explicitly warns that timed-out submissions can have reached the market. Resending under a new identity is therefore unsafe. Its initial crypto fee tier is **0.15% maker or 0.25% taker per side**, and a marketable limit removes liquidity. A gross 0.3% scalp fails a roughly 0.5% two-taker fee hurdle before spread and slippage. Fees charged in the received asset also affect sellable quantity. ([Order timeout guidance](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/working-with-orders), [crypto fees](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/crypto-fees))

A stop trigger can be deterministic while its eventual fill remains uncertain. Likewise, rejecting prompt instructions does not establish that a persuasive false article cannot influence an otherwise eligible ranking. General agent-security guidance supports structured boundaries and isolation but does not claim to eliminate injection risk. ([Agent safety guidance](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/agent-builder-safety))

## Current ASTRAL guards support a laboratory, not proven alpha

Read-only inspection confirms separate research screening, model review, timing decisions, and order risk. [Research screening](../backend/research.py) records facts, evidence, vetoes, timestamps, expiry, and a hash. Missing event-calendar coverage is unknown and vetoes entry; it is never interpreted as no upcoming event. The [CLI reviewer](../backend/codex_worker.py) disables tools, passes a minimal environment, limits attempts per database, and invalidates cached rationale when facts or source revisions change. Exact CLI dollar billing remains unknown. The [discovery module](../backend/universe.py) can represent additional venue-listed crypto without authorizing its execution.

The [engine](../backend/engine.py) reserves pending risk, isolates strategy inventory, retains uncertain order states, and reconciles activities. Its shadow mode cannot write broker orders. The [timing module](../backend/timing.py) uses explicit decision time, requires later quotes for synthetic fills, and labels profitability unvalidated. Relevant fixtures live in [selection/execution tests](../tests/test_dogfood.py), [timing tests](../tests/test_timing.py), and [worker/universe tests](../tests/test_worker_universe.py). This report does not assert a final test count while implementation continues.

The next evidence artifact should connect every proposed opportunity to its source snapshot, numerical signal, timing action, size and binding cap, counter-case, vetoes, eventual executable outcome, and net attribution. Record holds and rejected ideas too. Compare frozen crypto day/swing variants against cash, suitable BTC/ETH exposure, and simple deterministic rules at comparable risk. Attribute selection, market exposure, execution, fees, and operating expense separately. A model that increases market exposure during a rally must not receive a selection-skill label solely because total P&L rises; KTD-Fin demonstrates why that distinction matters in its own market. ([Attribution study](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.28359v1))

## Conclusion

The most valuable immediate improvement is a failure-to-control ledger with reproducible fixtures and explicit remaining gaps. Passing those fixtures establishes behavior under the tested contracts; it does not supply a winning probability, complete news coverage, an exit guarantee, or a profitable strategy. Keep the numerical authority small enough to audit and the research model constrained enough to replace.

For meme coins, opportunity should earn eligibility through verified identity, liquidity, data, costs, and exit feasibility. For learning, promotion should earn authorization through frozen forward evidence. Those requirements let ASTRAL investigate aggressive opportunities without confusing an enthusiastic narrative, a successful model call, or a rising market with demonstrated skill.
